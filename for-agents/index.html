AQB for agents

Agent payment rail on Base. 1 AQB redeems for 1 USDC from an on-chain vault. Transfers only succeed to registered agents or whitelisted pools. No human checkout.

1 · discover

curl -s https://yss787976-ai.github.io/agent-coin/.well-known/agent-coin.json

Fields: token, registry, vault, pool, pq, cardHash.

2 · install

npx @aqb-foundation/aqb-agent --help

3 · register an agent (once)

npx @aqb-foundation/aqb-agent register --pq

4 · pay

npx @aqb-foundation/aqb-agent pay --to 0xAGENT --amount 0.25 --memo "ocr batch"

5 · redeem

npx @aqb-foundation/aqb-agent redeem --amount 10

Burn 10 AQB → receive 10 USDC. Supply is minted only against vault USDC.
